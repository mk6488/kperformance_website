What this page does: calculates limb symmetry index, relative force (%BM), strength ratios, maturity offset (Mirwald et al., 2002), and flags results against fixed thresholds (90% LSI, 10% trial variance) — the same maths as the Excel calculator. Athlete names, dates of birth and longitudinal records stay in the private Excel workbook, not here — copy the numbers across using the button above.
