## 0:45–0:50 — Discussion

- What's improved, named specifically
- What still needs work
- Next block, or close out if this was the final retest

**Not repeated at retest** (unless clinically indicated or specifically flagged last time): PAR-Q, full injury/training history, maturation calculation, movement screen, overhead squat, balance test.

**Why:** these are unlikely to change meaningfully in eight weeks, and re-running them turns a 45-minute retest back into a 90-minute session — which is what makes the £180 package unprofitable.
